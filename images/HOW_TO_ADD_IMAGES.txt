HOW TO ADD YOUR IMAGES
======================

images/ folder structure
--------------------------
images/
├── hero/               ← Homepage hero background
│   └── hero-bg.jpg
│
├── categories/         ← Category card images (shown on homepage grid)
│   ├── cat-totes.jpg
│   ├── cat-clutch.jpg
│   ├── cat-backpack.jpg
│   ├── cat-crossbody.jpg
│   ├── cat-travel.jpg
│   └── cat-mini.jpg
│
├── products/           ← Product images (shown on category pages)
│   ├── totes/
│   │   ├── prod-tote-1.jpg
│   │   └── prod-tote-2.jpg
│   ├── clutch/
│   │   ├── prod-clutch-1.jpg
│   │   └── prod-clutch-2.jpg
│   ├── backpack/
│   │   └── prod-backpack-1.jpg
│   ├── crossbody/
│   │   └── prod-crossbody-1.jpg
│   ├── travel/
│   │   └── prod-travel-1.jpg
│   └── mini/
│       └── prod-mini-1.jpg
│
└── about/              ← About section image
    └── about.jpg


HOW TO REPLACE AN IMAGE
--------------------------
1. Drop your image into the correct subfolder above.
2. Open js/content.js and update the image path.

Examples:
  Hero:       backgroundImage: "images/hero/my-hero.jpg"
  Category:   image: "images/categories/cat-totes.jpg"
  Product:    image: "images/products/totes/my-tote.jpg"
  About:      image: "images/about/my-photo.jpg"

HOW TO ADD MORE PRODUCT IMAGES
--------------------------
1. Drop the image into the right products/[category]/ folder.
2. In js/content.js, find the product and update its image path.
   Example: image: "images/products/totes/new-tote.jpg"

RECOMMENDED SIZES
--------------------------
  Hero background  : 1920 x 1080 px
  Category cards   :  800 x  600 px
  Product images   :  800 x  600 px
  About section    :  900 x  675 px

SUPPORTED FORMATS
--------------------------
  .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp  .gif

NOTE: If an image is missing or the path is wrong, the website
automatically shows an emoji fallback — nothing will break.
